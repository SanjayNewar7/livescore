Privacy Policy for LiveGoal
Last updated: 31 July 2026

Thank you for using LiveGoal (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimal information necessary to provide and improve the App’s features. This includes:

a. Information You Provide Voluntarily
Profile Information: If you choose to set up a profile, we store your name, a short bio, and optionally a profile photo (which you select from your device’s gallery or camera).
Favorites: You can mark teams, leagues, and players as favorites. These preferences are stored locally on your device and are used to personalise the content you see (e.g., showing your favourite teams’ matches, generating notifications about them).
Preferences: App settings light mode, notification preferences, and last‑selected league.

b. Information Collected Automatically
Device Information: We collect non‑personal information about your device, such as model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers, to help us troubleshoot and improve performance.
Usage Data: We may collect anonymised usage statistics (e.g., which screens you visit, how often you use certain features) to understand how users interact with the App.

c. Third‑Party Data
The App displays live sports data, news, and statistics from third‑party APIs, including ESPN and API‑Football. We do not control what data those services collect; however, we only request publicly available data (scores, standings, player stats, news articles) and do not send any personally identifiable information to them.

2. How We Use Your Information
To provide, personalise, and improve the App’s functionality.
To remember your favourite teams, leagues, and players so that you can quickly access them.
To send push notifications about matches involving your favourite teams (e.g., final scores, goal alerts) – only if you have granted notification permissions.
To respond to your support requests and feedback.
To comply with legal obligations.
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security
Most of your data (profile, favourites, preferences) is stored locally on your device using a secure local database (Hive). We do not upload this data to our own servers.
Your profile photo, if you choose one, is stored only on your device’s local storage.
When you use third‑party services (e.g., ESPN, API‑Football), the data is transmitted over HTTPS and is not stored by us beyond what is necessary to display it within the App.
We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third‑Party Services
The App integrates the following third‑party services:

Service	Purpose	Privacy Policy
ESPN API	Provide live scores, standings, news, and player information.	ESPN Privacy Policy
API‑Football	Provide detailed player statistics, league leaders, and team data.	API‑Football Privacy Policy
TheSportsDB	Provide player headshot images as a fallback for ESPN.	TheSportsDB Privacy Policy
These third‑party services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. The App does not share any of your personal data with these services – only publicly available requests (e.g., a player’s ID or a match ID) are sent to retrieve public data.

5. Permissions We Use
Camera: We request camera permission only if you choose to take a new profile photo directly from the App. You can deny this permission and still use all other features.
Storage/Photos: We request storage permission to allow you to select an existing profile photo from your gallery. On Android 13 and above, we only request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES.

Notifications: We request permission to send you push notifications about your favourite teams’ match results and news. You can enable/disable this at any time in the App’s settings or your device’s system settings.
All permissions are optional and you may revoke them at any time through your device settings.

6. Children’s Privacy
The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights and Choices
Access and Correction: You can view and modify your profile information, favorites, and preferences directly within the App.
Delete Data: You can delete your profile photo or clear your favorites at any time. To delete all locally stored data, you can uninstall the App – this will remove all your stored preferences from your device.
Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent for notifications or camera access via your device settings.
Data Portability: Since all data is stored locally, you are in full control of it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the “Last updated” date. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.
